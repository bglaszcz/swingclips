How to find the perfect spot
  1. Stand at the ball at address with a 7 iron: the outline and meters below each picture judge where you are in it. Pictures come about once a second while the phones aren't recording.
  2. Distance: move the tripod until Size is in the green: big enough to measure, with room above your head for the club at the top and the finish.
  3. Level the phone side to side (3D needs it): choose Tap: level and tap the bottom and top of something upright in the picture (a door frame, a post, the edge of the net frame). Turn the phone the way it says until it reads level.
  4. On the line: lay an alignment stick on the mat pointing straight at the phone. Down the line: along the target line through the ball (or along your hand line, if that's where you want the phone). Face on: from the ball, square to the target line (lay a second stick on the target line to square it up). Choose Tap: stick and tap the stick's near and far ends: it looks perfectly upright only when the phone is right on its line. Move the tripod the way it says. Type each phone's lens height for the inches.
  5. Aim: the stick should also run up the middle of the picture (the grid's centre line), with you framed as the meters like.
  6. Save this spot for each phone, then tape the tripod's feet. Next time, tick Saved spot: the saved picture shows over the live one, and the match line says when you're back on it (the same check the server uses to notice a moved camera).
