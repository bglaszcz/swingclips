Key positions
Swing numbers
Face-on: turns are from how much narrower the hips and shoulders look than at address; inches are scaled from the body's estimated size. Both are estimates from one camera: best for comparing your own swings. Turn + = closed (going back), - = open; tilt + = lead side higher / spine away from the target; sway + = toward the target.
Down the line: "to ball" + = moved toward the ball since address (hips + at impact = early extension). Hand height is above the middle of the shoulders, hand depth behind them (away from the ball). The plane is the shaft's line at address: hands to plane + = above it; shaft to plane + = steeper than at address (-- where the shaft was a blur and couldn't be seen).
Session trends
One dot per swing; tap one to open it. The line is the straight-line fit. Launch monitor: left is -, right is + (path + = in-to-out, face + = open, for a right-hander).
r runs from -1 to +1: how closely the two go up and down together (0 = no link; - = one goes up as the other goes down). The tick marks how big it must be to stand out from chance with this many swings; below that it could easily be luck. Even one that stands out is a lead to test, not proof that one causes the other.

By session
Medians per group, measured by the server from each clip (no labels). Golfer: brightness at address, 0-255. Noise: grain in a still part of the background, in the same units (from about 2.5 it shows). Flicker: clips where the lights pulsed or banded. Sharp: detail round the hands and lower arms at address; P6 and P5-P7 are the same as a share of address in each clip, so clips in different light compare fairly: nearer 1 is sharper through the downswing. Green is the best setting for each camera. Clips from before capture app 0.4 are "unknown".
Practice
After each swing the speaking phone (Practice voice on, the face-on one by default) says the number and whether it was in range, e.g. "Tempo 3.2, in range" or "Club path minus 4, too far left". Body numbers come once the swing is analyzed (usually well under a minute); Square's about 15 s after the strike, or "no shot" if none came within 25 s. A body number from a camera that couldn't see all of you (the camera check) is "no reading", not a wrong number. Numbers marked "noisy" move a lot from one swing to the next even when the swing doesn't: practice them over several swings, not one.
