0Phones recording

Both phones need to be recording: they save a clip when they hear a clap. The clips taken here are left out of your trends.
3D is off on the server, so swings won't get 3D numbers yet. You can still calibrate here. To turn it on, add set SWINGCLIPS_3D=on to server\settings.cmd on the server and restart it.

3Camera positions (from your swings)

Once, after the tripods are on their spots, and again only if a tripod moves. Do the lens of both phones first.
  1. Set both tripods, then tap Tripods are set.
  2. Hit about 10 swings as usual (any club), and wait for them to be analyzed.
  3. Tap Place the cameras. It works out where each phone stands from your joints in both pictures, sized by your height. Check the heights and distances it prints with a tape measure. For the best size, measure the down-the-line phone's distance to the ball along the floor (from the spot under the phone) and type it in: it then sizes everything by that instead of your height.
Or with the mat board (the big printed board, if placing from swings doesn't work)

3Camera positions (mat board)

Once the tripods are in place. Do the lens of both phones first.
  1. Lay the mat board flat with its centre on the ball spot, the x arrow at the target and the y arrow away from you.
  2. Tap Start, step out of both pictures, clap once and wait 5 seconds.
  3. Tap Calibrate.
